Fiber prototypes

Two early tries at the fiber wing, for a knitter and weaver to poke at. Nothing here touches your Tincture save. Everything is in US terms.

Knitting Room

Knit stitch by stitch with real stitches, watch the fabric grow, and read it back as a written pattern or a chart.

  • Knit, purl, through the back loop, slips, decreases, increases, cables, brioche, short rows, bind off
  • Type a row like "k1, *yo, k2tog; rep from * to last st, k1"
  • Flip to the wrong side, see floats, knit in the round and view it as a tube

Loom

Draft a weave with threading, tie-up and treadling, then throw picks and watch the cloth grow.

Tell us what feels wrong to a knitter's or weaver's hands. That is the most useful thing.
