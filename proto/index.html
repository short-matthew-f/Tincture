Fiber prototypes

Early tries at the craft wing, for a fiber artist to poke at. Nothing here touches your Tincture save. Everything is in US terms.

The Tile Room

Draft glazed tiles from the dishes, fill pattern lines and set a mosaic on your wall. A gentle neighbour from next door shares the dishes.

Loom

Draft a weave with threading, tie-up and treadling, then throw picks and watch the cloth grow.

Marbling and origami

Float natural dyes on a bath, drag a stylus and comb through them, lay a sheet of paper, then fold your marbled paper into a cup, a fox or a tulip.

Dye Studio

Dye your own quilting fabric: solids and ombré, batik with wax and crackle, shibori, tie-dye, ice dye, marbled cloth and block prints. Everything goes into your stash.

Quilt Designer

Design blocks from the traditional library, lay out a quilt with sashing and borders, quilt it, and get a real cutting list and yardage. Uses the fabrics in your stash.

The Lifting Table

A painting comes into the workshop and you lift its pigments back out into jars. Choose which jars ride the belt; paint lifts from the bottom up. Twelve little pictures, no timers, undo is free.

Skein Stacks

Drag little piles of hand-dyed yarn onto a honeycomb of pegs. Matching colors slide together, and ten of a color twist into a labeled hank. Twelve levels, no fail, undo is free.

Tell us what feels wrong to a fiber artist's hands. That is the most useful thing.
