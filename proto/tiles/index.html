    How the Tile Room works
    1. Tap a tile in a dish: you take every tile of that colour. The others slide to the middle. You can also take a colour from the middle.
    2. Choose a pattern line. A line holds one colour, and not one already on that row of your wall.
    3. Extra tiles go to the seconds basket and are sold for +1 each.
    4. Ida next door takes a dish after each turn (most tiles of one colour, ties go left) and sells them.
    5. When the dishes are empty, full lines place one tile on the wall and score by neighbours. Finish a wall row to end the game.
