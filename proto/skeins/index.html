How this works
  1. Drag a stack from the tray onto any empty peg, or tap a stack and then a peg.
  2. Neighbors with the same top color slide together. What is underneath can slide on, so chains happen.
  3. When 10 or more of one color sit on top, they twist into a hank. Bundle enough hanks to finish the level. Board full? Undo as much as you like, or take a free nudge.
